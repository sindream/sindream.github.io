About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at KAIST. My research focuses on reinforcement learning for autonomous robotic systems, particularly unmanned aerial vehicles.

I am also interested in robotic perception, state estimation, and multi-robot systems. Before joining KAIST, I received my M.S. and B.S. degrees in Mechanical Engineering from UNIST in 2025 and 2023, respectively.
