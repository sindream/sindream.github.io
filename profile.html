Background

Autonomous systems / UAVs

Learning-driven robotics for dynamic, uncertain environments.

Woojae Shin is a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at KAIST. His primary research focuses on reinforcement learning for autonomous robotic systems, particularly unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). His broader research experience spans robotic perception, state estimation, and multi-robot systems.

His published work includes vision-based autonomous landing on moving platforms, radar-inertial odometry with online temporal calibration, and communication relay planning for dynamic indoor missions.
