About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at KAIST and a member of the Field AI and Robotics (FAIR) Laboratory. My research focuses on reinforcement learning for autonomous robotic systems, particularly unmanned aerial vehicles.

I am also interested in robotic perception, state estimation, and multi-robot systems. Before joining KAIST, I completed my M.S. at UNIST's Autonomous Systems Lab (ASL) in 2025 and received my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from UNIST in 2023.
