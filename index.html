Research statement

Perceive / Estimate / Act

Robots that remain capable when the world refuses to stay still.

My research explores how aerial and networked robotic systems can operate reliably in dynamic environments. I work across deep reinforcement learning, vision-based autonomy, state estimation, and communication-aware planning.

01 / 03UAV

Aerial autonomy

Vision-based control and deep reinforcement learning for landing UAVs on moving platforms, including platforms with uncertain motion.

Related paper ↗
02 / 03ESTIMATION

State estimation

Radar-inertial odometry with online temporal calibration for reliable motion estimation across asynchronous sensor streams.

03 / 03MULTI-ROBOT

Networked robotics
