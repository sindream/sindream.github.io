Research interests

My research focuses on learning and estimation for autonomous robotic systems, particularly UAVs working in dynamic environments.

Reinforcement learning for aerial robots

Learning-based control for autonomous UAV tasks, including landing on moving platforms with uncertain motion.

Vision-based autonomy

Using visual observations to support perception and decision-making for autonomous flight.

State estimation

Radar-inertial odometry and temporal calibration for reliable motion estimation.

Networked and multi-robot systems

Communication-aware planning for robots operating together in dynamic environments.
